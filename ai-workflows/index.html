Simulate Exam With Practice Questions
  1. Carry context forward: Find last relevant chat → create a summary → paste that summary into your new chat.
  2. Define the deliverable first: Mentally establish "I want a ___ by the end of this AI chat" before you start.
  3. Ask for an optimal prompt: Ask the AI how to write the best prompt for your specific outcome or deliverable.
  4. Speed over perfection: Follow optimal prompting guidelines, but prioritize momentum — avoid analysis paralysis.
