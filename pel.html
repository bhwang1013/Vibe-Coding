How to Play

• Pels (little squares) fall down one of three lanes: left, middle, or right.

• The paddle sits in one lane at a time. Move it with the left / right arrow keys, or tap a lane on mobile.

• Catch a pel with the paddle and it's collected — consecutive catches build your combo, and higher combos earn bonus points.

• Miss a pel and you lose a life and your combo resets. You start with 3 lives.

• Special boxes fall too — catch them for power-ups: ♥ extra life, ↔ wide paddle, ❄ slow motion, ×2 double points. Missing a box costs nothing.

• Difficulty comes in waves — survive the hard peaks and you get an easier breather before the next, tougher one.

• Earn badges: 2500 Bronze, 5000 Silver, 7500 Gold, 15000 Legendary.
