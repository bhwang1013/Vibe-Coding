How to Play

• Pels (little squares) fall from the top of the screen.

• Move the paddle with the left / right arrow keys (or touch and drag on mobile).

• Bounce every pel to score. Consecutive bounces build your combo — higher combos earn bonus points.

• If a pel falls past the paddle you lose a life and your combo resets. Lose all 3 lives and it's game over.

• The longer you survive, the faster and more numerous the pels become.
